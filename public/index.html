    How to play

    Collect number cards without drawing the same number twice. Bank your points before your luck runs out. First to 200 wins.

    Using this app

    Deal as normal with the real cards. As each card lands in front of you, tap it on your phone — everyone at the table sees the scores update. When the round is over, the host taps End round and every hand banks at once.

    Numbers you already hold are ringed on the keypad. Tapping a ringed number is your bust — the same thing that just happened on the table.

    The app shuffles and deals. On your turn, tap Hit for another card or Stay to bank what you're holding. When everyone has banked, busted or hit Flip 7, the host deals the next round.

    Draw a Freeze or Flip Three and you aim it yourself: tap a name on the scoreboard, then tap it again to confirm. The Bust-O-meter below your hand counts the exact remaining deck.

    Scoring

    • Bust — a second copy of a number you hold. Zero for the round.
    • Flip 7 — seven different numbers ends the round for everyone and pays +15.
    • +2 +4 +6 +8 +10 add on. ×2 doubles your number cards, not the bonuses. Modifiers can't bust you and don't count toward Flip 7.

    Action cards

    • Freeze — that player banks what they have and sits out the round. Their score is already correct in the app; just stop tapping.
    • Flip Three — they flip three cards. Tap them as they land.
    • Second Chance — tap the shield to hold one. Your next duplicate is discarded instead of busting you.

    The deck

    94 cards: one 0, one 1, two 2s, up to twelve 12s — plus six modifiers and nine action cards. High numbers are common, which is why a fat hand is a dangerous hand.
