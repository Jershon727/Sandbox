How to play

Collect number cards without drawing the same number twice. Bank your points before your luck runs out. First to 200 wins.

Your turn

  • Hit — flip one more card.
  • Stay — bank what you have and sit out the rest of the round.
  • Bust — flip a number you already have and you lose the whole round. Zero points.

Flip 7

Seven different number cards ends the round instantly and pays a +15 bonus. Everyone else scores whatever they're holding.

Modifiers

+2 +4 +6 +8 +10 add to your total. ×2 doubles your number cards (not the bonuses). Modifiers can't bust you and don't count toward Flip 7.

Action cards

  • Freeze — pick any player still in the round. They bank their points and sit out. Yes, you can pick yourself.
  • Flip Three — pick any player still in the round. They flip three cards, one at a time. Busting stops it early.
  • Second Chance — your next duplicate is discarded instead of busting you. Draw one while holding one and you must give it away.

The deck

94 cards: one 0, one 1, two 2s, all the way up to twelve 12s — plus six modifiers and nine action cards. High numbers are common, which is exactly why a fat hand is a dangerous hand.

House rule in this version: the deck is reshuffled at the start of every round, so the bust risk you see is always exact.
